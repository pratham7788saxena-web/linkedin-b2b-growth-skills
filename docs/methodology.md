# Methodology

## The ten stages

| Stage | Question it answers | Skills |
|---|---|---|
| 0. Set up | What can we truthfully say about ourselves? | `workspace-setup`, `linkedin-profile-optimizer` |
| 1. Learn | Do I understand what I'm marketing? | `topic-learning` |
| 2. Strategize | Which problem, for whom, with what objective? | `content-calendar` |
| 3. Find accounts | Which companies show evidence the problem is relevant? | `target-account-research` |
| 4. Find people | Who owns, influences or feels the problem? | `decision-maker-research` |
| 5. Qualify | Where does limited time go first? | `lead-qualification` |
| 6. Build visibility | Will they recognise us before we message? | `personal-linkedin-content`, `company-page-content`, `executive-ghostwriting`, `one-pager-creator`, `brand-creative-design` |
| 7. Engage | Can we add value in public first? | `linkedin-engagement` |
| 8–9. Connect & nurture | Is there a genuine reason to talk? | `linkedin-outreach` |
| 10. Track & improve | What moved, and what do we change? | `lead-tracker`, `weekly-review` |

## Evidence labels

| Label | Use for |
|---|---|
| `[VERIFIED]` + source | Facts from your own website or approved internal material |
| `[INDUSTRY]` | Public knowledge or a reliable third-party source |
| `[ASSUMPTION]` | A hypothesis to test |
| `[NEEDS APPROVAL]` | Results, customers, partners, numbers, executive voice |
| `[NEEDS RESEARCH]` | A gap, plus where to look |

## Scoring

**Accounts** (`target-account-research`): Fit · Relevance to a verified offering · Public evidence · Conversation opportunity, each 0–3. A = 10–12, B = 7–9, C = 4–6, D = 0–3.

**People** (`lead-qualification`): Company fit · Role relevance · Evidence of initiative · Ability to start a genuine conversation, each 0–3. P1 = 10–12, P2 = 7–9, P3 = 4–6, P4 = 0–3.

Scores rank *what to look at first*. They are not buying intent.

## Four kinds of metrics

| Group | Examples | Why separate |
|---|---|---|
| Activity | posts, comments, requests sent | Shows effort, not effect |
| Engagement | impressions, reactions, relevant engagement | Shows attention, not intent |
| Lead | acceptance rate, replies, conversations | Shows relationships moving |
| Business | meetings, opportunities handed to sales | The outcome that matters |

A campaign is judged on relevant engagement and qualified conversations, not likes or follower count.

## What counts as a qualified opportunity

Fit + relevant role + a need or initiative the person shared + interest in a next step. Agree the exact definition with sales. A connection, like or follow is never enough.

## Account coordination

| Account | Angle | Typical prospects |
|---|---|---|
| Executive | How leaders should think about the decision | Senior decision-makers |
| Company page | What the company does about it | Everyone (no outreach) |
| Personal | What I learned | Practitioners, managers, architects |

One owner per prospect. One theme per week, a different angle on each account.
