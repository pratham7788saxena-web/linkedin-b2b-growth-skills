# Content Calendar & Campaign Planner

**Purpose:** Make the work consistent and connected: one campaign theme, several angles, up to three accounts, plus engagement and outreach. Stage 2 (Strategize) and planning for Stages 6–9.

Read `references/ground-rules.md`, then `company-profile.md`, `campaign-log.md` and `content-log.md` in the workspace.

## When to use it

- Start of a campaign (after `topic-learning`): set the brief and plan.
- Weekly (e.g. Friday) to plan next week; monthly for the month.
- When priorities change mid-campaign or a second campaign starts sharing the same channels.

## Information to ask for

- The **campaign brief** (ground rules). If missing, build it first with the 9 questions. Don't plan without a confirmed focus, audience, problem and objective.
- Planning period and start date.
- Capacity: hours/week; who approves company posts and by when; whether an executive has agreed to post (include them only if yes).
- What's already published (`content-log.md`) and last week's `weekly-review`.
- Available assets (blogs, whitepapers, one-pagers, events).

## Workflow

### Step 1: Confirm the brief
Write it back in 9 lines. Flag [ASSUMPTION]s and approvals.

### Step 2: Campaign narrative
- One **theme** (e.g. "Close the books in days, not weeks").
- 3–4 **pillars/angles**, e.g. (a) the problem, (b) the concept, (c) what good looks like, (d) the company's verified capability + approved proof.
- An **arc** over the weeks: problem awareness → understanding → approach → conversation/asset.
- **Account roles:** executive = how leaders should think about the decision; company page = what the company does about it; personal = what I learned. Same weekly theme, different angle, different day.

### Step 3: Activities per week (adjust to capacity)
- Personal posts: 2/week
- Company page: 2–3/week
- Executive (if approved): 1–2/week, drafts sent for approval with lead time
- One longer piece (article/newsletter) every 1–2 weeks
- One asset per campaign or month
- Engagement: 15–20 min/day, ~5 meaningful comments
- Outreach: 10–20 personalised requests/week to qualified people; follow-ups as due
- Research: 3–5 accounts or 5–10 people/week

If two campaigns share the company page, split slots (e.g. campaign A Tue/Thu, campaign B Mon/Wed/Fri) and keep one master calendar.

### Step 4: Fill the calendar
Every row: **Date · Channel · Audience · Topic · Customer problem · Main message · Format · CTA · Research/approval needed · Pillar · Skill · Status.**

### Step 5: Anti-repetition check
- No two posts in a week with the same angle and format.
- Personal, company and executive posts on one theme use different angles and wording.
- Rotate formats (text, carousel/document, diagram image, poll, article, video if available).
- Check the last 4 weeks of `content-log.md`, including hooks and examples. If a message has already led three posts, don't open another post with it.

### Step 6: Dependencies and approvals
What needs research or approval, from whom, with a "needed by" date. Time-sensitive facts get a "re-check the day before" note.

## Output format

```
# [Campaign] — Plan [period]
## Campaign brief (9 lines)
## Theme, pillars, arc, account roles
## Calendar
| Date | Channel | Audience | Topic | Customer problem | Main message | Format | CTA | Research / approval | Pillar | Skill | Status |
## Engagement & outreach plan (weekly targets + which accounts)
## Approvals & research needed (item · owner · needed by)
## How we'll measure (link to weekly-review metrics)
## Record this (campaign-log decisions)
## Next step
```

For a spreadsheet, produce an .xlsx or fill the "Content Calendar" sheet of the `lead-tracker` template.

## Quality checks

- Every item ties to the theme and a specific audience + problem.
- All columns filled; nothing vague like "post about AI".
- Volume fits capacity: fewer, better items.
- Executive items only with confirmed permission, marked "draft for approval".
- Approvals scheduled with enough lead time.
- No repetition across channels or weeks.

## Example prompts

- "New campaign: accounts-payable automation for mid-size manufacturers in the UK, goal 10 meaningful conversations in 6 weeks. Plan the first 4 weeks."
- "Plan next week. Here's last week's review."
- "We're running two campaigns on one company page. Merge them into one calendar."

## Common mistakes to avoid

- Disconnected topics ("Mon: AI, Tue: hiring…").
- Planning more than can be researched and approved.
- Assuming the focus offering.
- Only posting, with no engagement or outreach.
- Using an executive's profile without permission.
